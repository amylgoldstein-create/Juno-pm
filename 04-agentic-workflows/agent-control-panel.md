## What Juno Is Doing

Insight synthesis: Juno checks each new ticket and customer signal for duplicates, groups matching tickets into clusters, and drafts new tickets for issues that aren't tracked yet, for a PM to approve

## Four Levers

**Stop Conditions** (Loop):

max_steps: 6 tool calls per ticket (2 passes, 3 calls each)
Abort early if:
- the same tool fails 2 times in a row
- a required source (Jira or CRM) is unreachable
- the cluster grows past 10 tickets, which likely means an incident, so it goes straight to the PM
Hard timeout: 60 seconds wall clock per ticket.
When it stops for any reason, the ticket goes into the PM panel as "Needs your review," with the reason it stopped.

**Structured Tool Outputs** (Tools):

search_similar_tickets
- Works: list of matching ticket IDs, a match count, and which fields matched (symptom, product area, release, date)
- Fails: status = error, with the error message
- Zero matches is a valid result, not a failure. It means this may be a new issue.

read_tickets
- Works: ticket fields (title, description, product area, release, created date, status)
- Fails: status = error. If any required field is blank, status = incomplete, plus a list of the missing fields.

read_account_from_crm
- Works: account name and renewal date (or "none on file")
- Fails: status = error. Juno doesn't fill in account names on its own and doesn't use account counts in that cluster.

read_signals (support tickets, Slack threads, interview notes)
- Works: list of signals with source, date, and account, plus a count
- Fails: status = error, naming which source failed

draft_cluster_suggestion / draft_new_ticket
- Works: draft ID, confidence level (High / Medium / Low), and the list of source IDs used
- Fails: status = rejected, with the reason. Example: "only 1 signal found"

**Confidence Thresholds** (Verification):

Duplicate clusters:
- High: tickets match on 3 or 4 of these: symptom, product area, release, created within 14 days. Shows in the panel automatically with boxes pre-checked. PM clicks to link.
- Medium: tickets match on 2. Shows as "Possible match" with boxes unchecked. PM decides.
- Low: fewer than 2 matches. Not suggested. Counted in "held back," with the reason.

New ticket drafts:
- High: issue appears in 2+ signals from 2+ accounts, and no existing ticket covers it. Draft shows in the panel for PM approval.
- Medium: 2+ signals from 1 account. Goes on a watch list, no draft yet.
- Low: 1 signal. No draft. Juno keeps watching for repeats.

Nothing is written to Jira at any level without a PM click.

**North Star** (Context):

Help the PM see which customer issues are really the same problem, and which ones aren't being tracked, so nothing important gets buried in the backlog.
Always:
- show the ticket IDs and signals behind every suggestion
Never:
- change anything in Jira without a PM click
- make up a customer name, count, or date
- group tickets with different release versions

## Rules of Engagement

**Agency Permission:**

Juno can do these on its own:
- read new tickets and signals from approved sources
- search for duplicates and compare tickets
- decide confidence level (High, Medium, Low)
- draft cluster suggestions and new tickets in the PM panel
- hold back weak matches and log why

Juno proposes and waits for a PM click:
- linking tickets as duplicates
- changing a ticket's status to Duplicate
- creating a new ticket in Jira

Juno can never:
- close a ticket
- change priority, assignee, or sprint
- edit the roadmap
- post in Slack, email, or comment on tickets

**Access Control:**

Juno runs on its own service account scoped to the RocketShip Platform Jira project only. It doesn't see every project in the company.
Read-only access to:
- support tickets
- CRM
- Slack product and support channels
- user interview notes
Write access in Jira is limited to linking tickets, setting status to Duplicate, and creating tickets, and only after a PM approves.
No access to:
- Slack DMs or private channels
- personal email
- billing, contracts, or HR data
- other Jira projects
Every action Juno takes is logged with the PM who approved it.

**Fallback Protocols:**

Hits 6 steps: stops and puts the ticket in the panel as "Needs your review," with what it found so far.
- Same tool fails 2 times in a row: stops trying. The panel shows "Couldn't check [source]." It tries once more on the next 15-minute run. If that fails too, it's flagged for the PM. No third retry.
- Jira or CRM is down: no suggestions that depend on that source. A banner shows which source is down.
- 60-second timeout: stops and hands back to the PM, same as hitting max steps.
- Cluster grows past 10 tickets: stops clustering and moves it to the top of the panel as a possible incident.
Juno never drops a ticket silently. Every stop shows up in the panel with a reason.

**Checkpoints:**

- Before any change in Jira: a PM approves every link, status change, and new ticket.
- Escalations: if a cluster involves a renewal in the next 30 days, data loss, or a security issue, the PM reviews it before Juno does anything else with that cluster.
- Weekly audit: the PM reviews 10 random approved clusters to catch bad merges that slipped through.
- Before more autonomy: Juno doesn't move to auto-linking until PMs approve 95% of High clusters without changes for 4 weeks straight, and the product lead signs off.
- Outside the company: none needed. Juno can't send anything outside RocketShip.
