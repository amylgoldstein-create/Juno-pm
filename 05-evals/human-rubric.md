## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Insight synthesis: Juno finds duplicate tickets in the Jira triage queue and suggests clusters for a PM to approve.
- **Value proposition:** Juno groups tickets that describe the same problem, shows me why, and lets me approve or reject a cluster in under 30 seconds without opening every ticket.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy - Grouping  | Do all tickets in the cluster describe the same underlying problem? | Yes / No | At least one ticket would need a separate fix, even if it sounds similar. | n/a | One fix would solve every ticket in the cluster |
| Accuracy - Omissions | Did Juno miss any ticket that clearly belongs in the cluster? | Yes / No | I found no open tickets that belong but were left out. | n/a | After 2 minutes of searching the queue, I found at least one open ticket that clearly belongs. |
| Justification (deal-breaker) | How clearly does Juno explain why these tickets belong together? | 1 to 5 | The reason is wrong or doesn't match what the tickets say. | Accurate but general, like "similar pipeline issue." I'd need to open one ticket to be sure. | Names the specific shared details (symptom, release, timing), and all of them check out. I could approve without opening a ticket. |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** 10 suggestions every week: 5 random approved clusters and 5 Medium-confidence suggestions
- **Who scores them with you:** One other PM. We each score all 10 on our own
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** We talk it through. If we still don't agree, the product lead makes the call, and we add that example to our practice set so it doesn't come up again.

## Pass bar

Yes on at least 9, No on at least 8, average of 4 or higher

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Justification: any score of 1
- Any change made in Jira without a PM approval. A Slack DM or private customer detail shows up in a suggestion.
