## Problem & user

RocketShip PMs need this handled: CANDIDATE Juno works in the Jira triage queue to find duplicate tickets, draft new tickets from untracked customer signals, and recommend backlog priority based on product strategy and engineering capacity. A PM approves anything before it changes in Jira. Juno does this inside limits a human set on purpose, and defends every call it makes.

## 01 Context · Data Requirements

**Required sources, and exclusions**

Juno uses only these sources:
- Jira tickets in the RocketShip Platform project 
- Jira Support tickets
- CRM rep notes and customer asks
- Slack threads in the product and support channels
- User interview notes
- Product Strategy One-Pager, to tie priority to company goals
- Engineering Capacity Notes, to judge what fits this sprint and the next
- Product Roadmap, to see what's already committed
Excluded on purpose:
- Slack DMs and private channels
- Personal email inboxes
- Billing data, contracts, and HR data
- Any source not on this list

**How fresh must it be**

How fresh must it be
- New tickets and signals are picked up within 15 minutes.
- Engineering Capacity Notes must be from the current or last sprint. If they're older, Juno still ranks by strategy but won't call anything a quick win.
- If the Strategy One-Pager or Roadmap is more than 90 days old, Juno notes that next to every priority recommendation.
- If a source is down, the panel says which one. Juno won't make a recommendation that depends on it.

## 02 Tools · System Capabilities

**The verb list**

read_tickets: read
- search_similar_tickets: read
- read_signals (support, CRM, Slack, interviews): read
- read_strategy_roadmap_capacity: read
- draft_cluster_suggestion: draft
- draft_new_ticket: draft
- draft_priority_recommendation: draft
- link_as_duplicate: write (after PM approval)
- set_status_duplicate: write (after PM approval)
- create_ticket: write (after PM approval)
- set_priority: write (after PM approval)
- save_pm_feedback: write

**Deliberate omissions**

- No close_ticket. Closing hides a ticket from everyone, and a mistake is easy to miss.
- No change_assignee or add_to_sprint. Deciding who works on what, and when, belongs to the PM and tech lead.
- No edit_roadmap. Juno can recommend that something move up, but the roadmap is a commitment to leadership and customers.
- No post_to_slack, email, or comment_on_ticket. Juno shouldn't speak for the PM or imply a fix is coming.

## 03 Loop · AI Costs & Latency

**Turn ceiling and escalation**

- Juno gets 2 passes per task (cluster, new ticket, or priority recommendation), with up to 3 tool calls per pass. That's 6 calls max.
- If confidence is still below High after 2 passes, it shows the item as "Needs your review" with what it couldn't confirm.
- It escalates to the top of the panel right away if:
  - a cluster has more than 10 tickets (likely an incident)
  - an issue affects an account with a renewal in the next 30 days
  - signals point to data loss or a security problem
  - a high-impact issue doesn't map to any current strategy goal (the PM needs to decide if strategy is missing something)

**Latency and cost target**

- Clusters, draft tickets, and priority recommendations are built in the background, so the panel loads in under 2 seconds.
- A manual refresh comes back in under 15 seconds.
- Cost stays under $0.03 per ticket or signal processed, so a full backlog review has a known, fixed cost.

## 04 Memory · Data Requirements

**What persists, at what scope**

- which tickets and signals Juno already processed, so they aren't treated as new
- approved and rejected clusters, with reasons
- draft tickets the PM rejected, with reasons
- priority recommendations the PM changed, and what they changed them to
Nothing crosses into other Jira projects or teams. Nothing from DMs or private channels is ever stored

**Expiry and write rules**

- "Already processed" markers last 90 days.
- A rejected cluster or draft ticket isn't suggested again unless new signals show up for it.
- Priority recommendations expire at the end of each sprint and are rebuilt from fresh capacity notes.
- PM feedback notes last 180 days.
- A human decision always beats Juno's output. If a PM changes a priority or unlinks a ticket, Juno accepts it and doesn't push back the next day.

## 05 Permissions · AI Risks & Mitigations

**The permission tier**

| Side-effect class | Tier |
|---|---|
| read | auto |
| draft | auto |
| write | confirm |
| send | blocked |

**Justification**

- Read is auto. Reading approved sources changes nothing.
- Draft is auto. Drafts only show in Juno's panel. A wrong draft costs the PM one click to reject.
- Write is confirm. Linking, creating tickets, and setting priority change what engineering and leadership see. They can be undone, but a wrong priority can send a team after the wrong problem for a sprint before anyone notices.
- Send is blocked. Messages to customers, sales, or support can't be taken back and could sound like a commitment.

## 06 Verification · AI Testing & Measurement

**The check before output ships**

Duplicate clusters:
- Each ticket must match the others on at least 2 of: same symptom, same product area, same release, created within 14 days.
- 3 or more matches is High (pre-checked). 2 matches is "Possible match" (unchecked). Fewer isn't shown.

New tickets:
- Juno searches existing tickets first, so it never drafts a new ticket for something already tracked.
- The issue must show up in at least 2 signals or come from 2 accounts.
- Every draft lists its source signals and fills in the required fields: title, product area, accounts, description.

Priority recommendations:
- Each recommendation names the strategy goal it supports, the number of accounts affected, any renewal risk, and the effort estimate from the capacity notes.
- Juno can only call something a "quick win" if effort is listed as small in the capacity notes and it fits without pushing back committed roadmap work.
- If the effort estimate is missing, Juno says so and doesn't guess.

Targets, tracked weekly:
- PMs approve 80% of High clusters without changes.
- PMs keep 70% of draft tickets with only minor edits.
- PMs accept 70% of priority recommendations as-is.

**Failure behaviour**

If a check fails, Juno shows it rather than hiding it. Example: "1 draft ticket held back: only one signal found."
- If any target is missed 2 weeks in a row, that feature switches to "suggest only, no pre-filled values" until the rules are reviewed.
- A recommendation is never shown without its reasons. If Juno can't explain it, it doesn't show it.
